UMPOFFICIAL RULES

Last updated October 28, 2026

This is the personal site of Jeffrey Woodbury, an individual, not a business. It has no accounts, no forms, no payments, no newsletter and no ads.

PRIVACY

I don't collect any personal information through this site. There are no analytics, tracking pixels, embedded videos, social widgets or other third-party scripts. Every file, including the font, is served from this site, so your browser doesn't contact anyone else while you read it. The optional baseball game is a small script served from this site too. It runs entirely in your browser, stores nothing and sends nothing.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical details of each visit, such as your IP address, for security and to keep the service running. I don't have access to those logs. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement (opens in a new tab) for how GitHub handles them.

If you email me, I receive only what you choose to send. I use it to reply to you and nothing else. I don't sell it, share it or add you to any list.

COOKIES

This site sets no cookies and uses no browser storage, so there is no cookie banner. If that ever changes, I'll ask for your consent first and update this page.

YOUR DATA

The only data I could hold about you is an email you sent me. To have it deleted, email jeffrey.w.woodbury@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request". I'll delete our correspondence and confirm within 30 days.

CHILDREN

This site is meant for employers and other professionals. It doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

ACCESSIBILITY

I aim for this site to meet WCAG 2.2 level AA. Everything works with a keyboard alone. The lineup card next to the ballpark lists every section and profile link, and this page is linked from the footer. The drawing of the field is a mouse and touch shortcut to the same places. There's a skip link and a visible focus outline, the short pitch animation turns off when your system asks for reduced motion, and text contrast is 4.5:1 or higher. Body text uses your device's own fonts, and the pixel font is used only for headings and labels.

The baseball game is optional. Start it with the Play Ball button under the field. It only reads keys while it has focus, so the page scrolls normally the rest of the time. Tab moves out of it, Escape quits, and on touch screens it has its own buttons. Everything the fielders tell you is also on the page itself.

If something doesn't work for you, please email jeffrey.w.woodbury@gmail.com and I'll fix it.
