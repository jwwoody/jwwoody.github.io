CABOUT ME

I build streaming video apps and the services behind them. Day to day I work on Roku, Apple TV, iPhone, iPad, Android and the web, plus the Node.js and AWS backend they run on.

Most of my work is live playback. I keep it working for BYUtv's 24/7 channel, which peaks above 170,000 concurrent viewers, and I'm usually the first one looking at the dashboards when something breaks on air. I also manage a small team of junior engineers.
